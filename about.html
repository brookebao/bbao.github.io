A little about me

Hi, I'm Brooke.

Hello! I am a dual degree student at Wellesley College and Dartmouth College with an interest in engineering, design, and data science. Being a dual degree student means I had the opportunity to study and graduate from Wellesley College in three years with a B.A. in Data Science and minor in economics while also pursuing a B.E. in Mechanical Engineering at Dartmouth. I hope to explore how data can inform our design decisions and how the intersection of these fields can make our world better.

I am originally from Washington D.C., and in my free time I play piano, watch Formula 1, and am on the Dartmouth figure skating team. I have worked at Blank Street as a barista and you can frequently find me skateboarding around Boston.
